A LINK, IN FULL BLOOM

Give a link
a lovely entrance.

  1. Plant a link. Add any http or https address and choose your flowers.
  2. Watch it bloom. Click the bouquet. The petals arrange into a scannable QR code.
  3. Pass it on. Share the bouquet link, or save the floral QR as an image.

The bouquet link holds your design. The QR opens your destination. No account needed.
